# Sochen is installed

**Free right away, no account:** ask Hermes for a GST invoice, monthly rent receipts for HRA, or a
bill split with a UPI QR for each person.

**Hand Sochen the jobs Hermes can't do alone** (phone a business, research that keeps running in the
cloud, email and calendar jobs that wait for your OK):

1. Make a key at https://sochen.dev/hermes. A new account gets 100 free credits, spread over any
   number of jobs.
2. Run `hermes config set SOCHEN_API_KEY <your key>`, or add it in Hermes Desktop under
   Settings → Plugins → Sochen.
3. Ask Hermes, or type `/sochen <a job>`. `/sochen` on its own shows your desk.

Hermes asks before it hands Sochen a job that uses your credits. Anything that pays, books, sends
or deletes waits for your OK in the Sochen app.
